# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Trailhead to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, identity, or background.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Stay on topic. PRs and issues are about the addon.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer at the email in `Trailhead.toc`.
Responses range from a warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion.
